# Community conduct

SubBurn's project spaces are for technical collaboration. Participants are expected to:

- discuss people and ideas respectfully;
- critique code and evidence rather than individuals;
- avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized conduct, doxxing, spam, and deliberate disruption;
- avoid publishing another person's private information, credentials, media, logs, or security details without permission;
- follow maintainers' reasonable moderation and security instructions.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or the project. Serious security or privacy concerns should be handled through the private process in `SECURITY.md`, not litigated in public threads.
